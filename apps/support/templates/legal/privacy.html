1. INTRODUCTION

Welcome to JaraMarket.

JaraMarket (“JaraMarket”, “Jara”, “we”, “us” or “our”) is a digital food ingredient sourcing, recipe discovery and marketplace platform designed to help individuals, households, businesses and other customers identify what they want to cook and obtain the ingredients required through participating vendors.

Our services are provided through our website, mobile applications, associated digital platforms and other approved communication channels.

This Privacy Policy explains how JaraMarket collects, receives, processes, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information relating to customers, prospective customers, vendors, vendor representatives, delivery partners, business partners and visitors to our platforms.

By accessing or using JaraMarket, creating an account, submitting an order, registering as a vendor or interacting with our services, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to read this Privacy Policy.

Where applicable law requires consent, we will obtain that consent separately and in an appropriate manner.

Our privacy practices are guided by the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, applicable regulations, consumer protection requirements and other relevant laws.

2. IDENTITY AND ROLE OF JARAMARKET

JaraMarket operates a digital platform that facilitates connections between customers, participating vendors and delivery service providers.

Depending on the particular service and transaction, JaraMarket may act as:

  1. A digital marketplace operator.
  2. A platform facilitating the sourcing of food ingredients and groceries.
  3. An intermediary coordinating customer orders and vendor fulfilment.
  4. A technology service provider.
  5. A provider of recipe discovery and meal recommendation tools.
  6. A coordinator of delivery requests through authorised logistics partners.
  7. An administrator of customer accounts, rewards and platform-related wallet records.

JaraMarket does not necessarily manufacture, grow, package, inspect, own, prepare or physically handle every product displayed or supplied through its platform.

The actual role and responsibility of JaraMarket in each transaction will depend on the service offered, the transaction structure and applicable law.
